Gentle routines and practical product types — no huge product collection needed.

Morning skincare

Keep it simple and consistent.

  • Gentle cleanser or water if appropriate for your skin
  • Moisturiser that suits your skin
  • Broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen
Gentle cleanserFragrance-free, non-scrubbing
MoisturiserChoose based on skin comfort
SPF 30+ sunscreenBroad-spectrum; follow label directions
🌙

Evening skincare

Cleanse, moisturise, and give skin time to rest.

  • Gently wash away sunscreen and dirt
  • Pat skin dry with a clean towel
  • Apply a comfortable moisturiser
  • Avoid harsh scrubs and picking spots
Gentle cleanserHelps remove daily buildup
Basic moisturiserLook for one that doesn't sting
💅🏾

Nail-care routine

Healthy-looking nails begin with gentle habits.

  • File rough edges gently
  • Moisturise hands and nail edges
  • Use polish if you like
  • Keep nail tools clean and don't share them
Nail fileGentle shaping
Hand creamHelps moisturise hands
Cuticle oilOptional; moisturiser works too
Base/top coatOptional polish extras
Skin and nail safety: products aren't one-size-fits-all. Patch-test new products, introduce one at a time, follow the label, and stop if a product causes burning, swelling or a rash. Avoid cutting cuticles. For persistent or painful problems, ask a trusted adult and healthcare professional. This site doesn't diagnose skin conditions.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for first-time visitors.

Is Braid & Bloom free to use?

Yes. You can browse the tutorials, try the style finder, save favourite looks and use the daily checklist for free.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed. Favourites and progress are saved in this browser on this device, so they may not follow you to another device or browser.

Are the hairstyle photos exact instructions?

Photos are inspiration. Follow the written steps and linked tutorials, and ask a trusted adult or stylist for help if you're unsure.

What if a hairstyle feels tight or hurts?

Loosen or remove the style. Comfortable styles should not cause pain or headaches, and it's okay to choose a different look.

Does Braid & Bloom diagnose skin or hair conditions?

No. The site offers general educational information only. For persistent irritation or concerns, speak with a trusted adult and a qualified healthcare professional.
